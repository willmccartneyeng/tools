Structural Stone Lintel Calculator

Method and assumptions

Allowable stress check of a plain stone lintel: bending, shear, bearing and deflection. Wall load is taken from arching action where the masonry above is deep enough to develop it, and in full where it is not. Allowable bending stress is the modulus of rupture divided by the factor of safety entered; use test data for the stone actually specified.
