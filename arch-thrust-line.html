Line of thrust in a masonry arch

Limit analysis by graphic statics. Enter a geometry and the tool finds the range of horizontal thrust for which a line of thrust fits inside the arch ring. Zero tensile strength, rigid blocks, and no sliding are assumed, after Heyman. Compressive stress at the joints is not checked.
